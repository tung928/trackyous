Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding package statuses and common delivery inquiries

What is the difference between "In Transit" and "Out for Delivery"?

In Transit means your parcel is traveling between distribution centers, airports, or freight hubs. Out for Delivery means the package has been loaded onto a local courier vehicle and will arrive at your destination address today.

Why hasn't the tracking status updated in 24-48 hours?

During long-distance transit (cross-country trucking or international maritime/cargo flights), packages do not get scanned until they arrive at the next major regional sort facility. This does not mean the package is lost.

Can I change my delivery address or instruct the driver where to leave the box?

Yes! You can use the "Delivery Note" button above to leave instructions (e.g. "Leave behind porch planter", gate code) or choose "Hold at Pickup Hub" to collect it in person at a nearby secure locker.
